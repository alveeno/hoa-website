The Critic

Erin Larkin

The Robert Parker Wine Advocate’s reviewer for Australia — and the critic whose verdict made history.

Who she is

An independent voice for Australian wine

Erin Larkin is an independent wine writer, judge and presenter based in Perth, Western Australia. In 2022 she was appointed the Robert Parker Wine Advocate’s first dedicated reviewer for Australian wines — the person responsible for tasting and rating Australia’s wines for the publication’s global audience.

Her background runs deep: years of tastings for the respected Halliday Wine Companion, a weekly wine column since 2014, and frequent judging at wine shows across Australia. In other words, she is a seasoned, professional palate — not a casual reviewer.

“
It is necessary for me to sit back and consider all the great Champagne prestige cuvées I have tasted from this vintage in either the northern or southern hemispheres, and I cannot bring to mind a better example.
Erin Larkin On the House of Arras E.J. Carr Late Disgorged 1998 Magnum

Read that again: a Wine Advocate critic compared this Tasmanian wine to the greatest prestige cuvées — the flagship bottlings — of Champagne, and could not think of a better one.

Why it counts

Why this verdict carries real weight

It’s independent

A Wine Advocate review is a critic’s professional judgment, not the winery’s marketing. That independence is the whole point — and why the trade trusts it.

It’s expert

Larkin has spent years tasting and judging at the highest level. A 100 from a palate this experienced is a considered verdict, not a fluke.

It’s global

Her scores are published to the Wine Advocate’s worldwide readership — collectors, sommeliers and retailers who act on them.

The reassurance, if you’re new to this

You don’t have to take a brand’s word that this wine is special. A respected, independent critic at the world’s most influential wine publication tasted it blind against the best in the world — and called it perfect.
