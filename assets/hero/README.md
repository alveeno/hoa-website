# Hero product assets — House of Arras E.J. Carr Late Disgorged 1998 Magnum

Faux-3D product set: three angle-consistent studio renders of the bottle
(same set, lighting, camera; bottle rotated on its vertical axis), shot in a
pale champagne-gold / ivory editorial style.

| `arras-bottle-right-master.png` | ~30° right | 1696×2528 master |
| `arras-bottle-{front,left,right}.webp` | — | 900w web derivative |
| `arras-bottle-{front,left,right}-lg.webp` | — | 1500w web derivative (retina) |

The home-page hero (`index.html` + `js/main.js` "bottle stage") crossfades
the three views and tilts the plate with pointer movement and scroll to
simulate a 3D turntable. Regenerate the `.webp` derivatives from the masters
at quality 84 if the masters are ever replaced.
